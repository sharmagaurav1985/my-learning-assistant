Terms of use

My Learning Assistant is an independent software-learning prototype developed by Gaurav Sharma.

Updated 6 October 2026

Current availability

This website explains a project in development. Publishing these pages does not provide access to a running Teams bot. The initial prototype is intended for personal developer testing with synthetic data.

Permitted use

Use the prototype only for authorized learning and testing. Do not attempt to bypass account permissions, access another person's data, misuse application APIs, or submit secrets or confidential information.

AI-generated responses and actions

When AI functionality is enabled, its responses may be incorrect or incomplete. Verify results before relying on them. Application access rules continue to apply to every requested action.

Availability and changes

The prototype may change, be interrupted, or be discontinued as development progresses. It has no production availability or support commitment. Do not use it for critical decisions or workflows.

Other services

Microsoft Teams, hosting services, and any AI provider operate under their own terms. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft.

Before broader use

Operational privacy details, a direct contact route, and updated terms must be established before the app is offered to other users.
